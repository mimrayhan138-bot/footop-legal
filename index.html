About FOOTOP IPTV PLAYER: FOOTOP IPTV PLAYER is a technical media player that allows users to access and play content from sources supplied or authorised by them, including M3U/M3U8 playlists and compatible Xtream-based services.

Footop does not provide, sell, host, distribute, or bundle channels, movies, playlists, subscriptions, or other media content. Footop is not affiliated with third-party content providers. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the legal right or authorisation to access and play the content they add to the app.

Information We Process

The app may process playlist and server URLs, provider usernames and passwords, playlist names and settings, and channel, category, programme and media metadata.

This information is used only to connect to the source selected by the user and to provide media playback.

Playlist information, provider settings and credentials may be stored locally in the app's private storage on the user's device.

We do not intentionally transmit saved playlists or provider credentials to Footop-operated servers.

Third-party content providers may receive information such as the user's IP address when the user connects to their services. Such processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy.

If you contact Footop by email, we may receive the name, email address, message and technical information that you voluntarily provide.

Advertising

The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process information such as advertising ID, IP address, device information, app information, and advertising interaction data in order to serve, measure and improve advertising and, where applicable, limit ad frequency.

This information is processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy .

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's applicable consent requirements may apply. Where required by applicable law, the app may request consent before showing personalised advertisements.

Users can also manage advertising personalisation and their advertising ID through their device settings.

Push Notifications

The app uses OneSignal to deliver push notifications, such as app updates or service announcements.

OneSignal may process a device push token, a OneSignal-generated subscription or player identifier, general device information such as device model and operating system version, app version, and notification interaction information such as whether a notification was received or opened.

This information is processed according to OneSignal's Privacy Policy .

Users can disable notifications through their device's notification settings.

How We Use Information

Information is used to load and play user-selected content, store application preferences, display and measure advertising, deliver push notifications, provide support, maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, improve application performance, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data or provider credentials.

The app uses third-party services including Google AdMob, Google services required for application functionality, and OneSignal. These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

Our website hosting provider may process limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the website, such as IP address, browser or device type, access times, and server or security logs.

Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, services, rights or safety.

Data Storage and Deletion

Users can remove locally stored information by deleting playlists within the app, clearing the app's storage through device settings, or uninstalling the application.
