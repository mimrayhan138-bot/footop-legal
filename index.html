Privacy Policy

Effective: September 1, 2026

About Footop IPTV Player: Footop IPTV Player lets users access and play content from sources supplied or authorised by them, including M3U/M3U8 playlists and compatible Xtream-based services. Footop does not provide, sell, host or distribute channels, movies, playlists, subscriptions or other media, and is not affiliated with third-party content providers. Users must access only content they are legally authorised to use.

Information we process

The app may process playlist and server URLs, provider usernames and passwords, playlist names and settings, and channel, category, programme and media metadata. This information is used only to connect to the source you select and provide playback. It is stored locally; Xtream credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore. We do not intentionally transmit saved playlists or credentials to Footop-operated servers.

Providers may receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. If you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, message and technical details you voluntarily provide. Our hosting provider may process IP address, browser or device type, access times, and server, error or security logs.

Advertising

The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and general usage and interaction data (for example, which ads are shown or clicked) in order to serve and measure ads and, where applicable, limit ad frequency.

This information is processed by Google according to Google's privacy policy and, for European Economic Area, UK and Swiss users, Google's EU user consent policy .

Where required by applicable law, the app will request your consent before showing personalised ads. You can also manage ad personalisation and your advertising ID through your device's settings.

Push notifications

The app uses OneSignal to deliver push notifications, for example app updates or service announcements. To do this, OneSignal may process a device push token, a OneSignal-generated subscription or player identifier, general device information such as device model and operating system version, app version, and notification interaction data such as whether a notification was received, opened, or dismissed.

This information is processed according to OneSignal's privacy policy . You can disable notifications at any time from your device's app notification settings.

How we use information

Information is used to load and play user-selected content, store preferences, show and measure advertising, deliver notifications, improve performance, respond to support requests, prevent fraud or abuse, protect security and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or playlist credentials.

Third-party services

User-selected playlists and accounts connect directly to third-party providers whose privacy, content, security and availability Footop does not control.

The app also relies on third-party services including Google Firebase services, Google AdMob, and OneSignal. These services may process information as described in their respective sections and according to their own privacy policies.

Our hosting provider may process limited technical data needed to run and secure our app. Information may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, services, rights or safety.

Data storage and deletion

Playlist information, provider settings and preferences may be stored locally on your device. You can remove them by deleting the playlist in the app, clearing app storage in device settings, or uninstalling the app.

No Footop account is required. To request deletion of information sent directly to us by email, contact us using the details below.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Xtream credentials are encrypted locally with Android Keystore. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13 or the applicable minimum digital-consent age. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

User responsibility

Users must have the legal right or authorisation to access and play all content added to the app, protect provider credentials, and use only trusted, authorised providers.

Footop is not responsible for third-party content, privacy practices, security or conduct.

International data processing

Google, OneSignal and our hosting provider may process information outside Bangladesh under their own privacy policies and applicable data-protection requirements.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact us below to exercise an applicable right.
