Effective: September 1, 2026

About Footop IPTV Player: Footop IPTV Player lets users access and play content from sources supplied or authorised by them, including M3U/M3U8 playlists and compatible Xtream-based services. Footop does not provide, sell, host or distribute channels, movies, playlists, subscriptions or other media, and is not affiliated with third-party content providers. Users must access only content they are legally authorised to use.

Information we process

The app may process playlist and server URLs, provider usernames and passwords, playlist names and settings, and channel, category, programme and media metadata. This information is used only to connect to the source you select and provide playback. It is stored locally; Xtream credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore. We do not intentionally transmit saved playlists or credentials to Footop-operated servers. Providers may receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. If you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, message and technical details you voluntarily provide. Our hosting provider may process IP address, browser or device type, access times, and server, error or security logs.

Analytics

The app sends a randomly generated device identifier, an event type (for example, app opened or screen viewed) and a screen name to our own analytics server. This helps us understand aggregate app usage and improve the app. This device identifier is not linked to your name, email address or any other personally identifiable information, and it is not shared with, sold to, or made accessible to third parties. The app currently does not display advertising and does not use any advertising SDK or advertising identifier.

How we use information

Information is used to load and play user-selected content, store preferences, improve performance, respond to support requests, prevent fraud or abuse, protect security and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or playlist credentials.

Third-party services

User-selected playlists and accounts connect directly to third-party providers whose privacy, content, security and availability Footop does not control. Our hosting provider may process limited technical data needed to run and secure our app. Information may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, services, rights or safety.

Data storage and deletion

Playlist information, provider settings and preferences may be stored locally on your device. Remove them by deleting the playlist in the app, clearing app storage in device settings, or uninstalling the app. No Footop account is required. To request deletion of information sent directly to us by email, contact us using the details below.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Xtream credentials are encrypted locally with Android Keystore and HTTPS is required for Xtream authentication. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13 or the applicable minimum digital-consent age. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

User responsibility

Users must have the legal right or authorisation to access and play all content added to the app, protect provider credentials, and use only trusted, authorised providers. Footop is not responsible for third-party content, privacy practices, security or conduct.

International data processing

Our hosting and analytics providers may process information outside Bangladesh under their own privacy policies and applicable data-protection requirements.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us below to exercise an applicable right.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.
